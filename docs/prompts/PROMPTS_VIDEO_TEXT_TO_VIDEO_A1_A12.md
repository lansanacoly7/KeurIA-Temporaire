# Pack Intégral : Prompts Vidéo Autonomes (Text-to-Video) — Modules a1 à a12
## Arrivées des parcours 10-12 ans à Dakar (`arrivee-a1.mp4` à `arrivee-a12.mp4`)

Ce guide regroupe les **60 prompts vidéo complets (12 modules x 5 plans)**, conçus pour fonctionner directement en génération vidéo (Flow, Runway Gen-3, Kling, Sora, Luma, etc.) **sans aucune dépendance envers des images de référence ou photos préalables**.

---

### Structure commune verrouillée (injectée dans chaque prompt)
- **Style visuel & Animation :** 2D flat-vector animation cartoon style, clean dark-brown outlines, warm saturated colors, bright African daylight, 16:9 widescreen ratio, smooth natural motion.
- **Moussa (11 ans, 100%) :** Garçon sénégalais, t-shirt jaune vif, short cargo orange, sac à dos bleu roi, chaussettes blanches, baskets rouge-orange.
- **Fatou (11 ans, 97%) :** Fille sénégalaise, robe bleu roi, ruban vert retenant son chignon tressé, sacoche marron en bandoulière, sandales bleues.
- **Palou le lionceau (85% de Moussa) :** Pelage doré, crinière orange touffue (dont le haut arrive au nez de Moussa), losange blanc sur le poitrail, queue à pompon. Il se tient et marche **toujours debout sur deux pattes** (anthropomorphe, taille enfant, ni lion adulte ni bête à 4 pattes).
- **Papa Ibou (adulte, 125%) :** Chauffeur sénégalais bienveillant, casquette verte, chemise à carreaux orange et jaune, moustache noire soignée.
- **Le Car Rapide :** Minibus vintage dakarois emblématique, haut jaune, bas bleu ciel, frises décoratives et motifs géométriques naïfs peints, porte arrière battante, galerie de toit garnie de bagages. (Pour a7 : Chaloupe passager blanche à deux ponts de l'île de Gorée).

## Module a1 - Monument de la Renaissance africaine
**Lieu :** Esplanade dallee en pente douce menant au grand escalier en pierre et au colossal monument de bronze sur la colline.

### Plan 1 : L'arrivée du Car Rapide (4 s)
```text
2D flat-vector cartoon animation style, clean dark outlines, bright African daylight, 16:9. Wide establishing shot of the African Renaissance Monument plaza in Dakar: expansive light stone paving leading straight toward the grand stone staircase and colossal bronze statue on the hill. An authentic Dakar Car Rapide (vintage minibus, yellow upper half, blue lower half, colorful painted geometric patterns, loaded roof rack) drives into frame from the bottom foreground, heading away from the camera, and comes to a smooth halt on the open paving left of center. No pedestrians outside. Smooth motion, camera static, 16:9.
```

### Plan 2 : La descente des enfants (4 s)
```text
2D flat-vector cartoon animation style, clean outlines, warm saturated colors. Medium shot facing the rear of the stopped yellow and blue Car Rapide on the paved monument esplanade. The rear door swings open. Moussa (11yo boy in yellow t-shirt and orange cargo shorts with blue backpack), Fatou (11yo girl in royal blue dress with green ribbon in braided hair), and Palou (young lion with golden fur, orange mane, white chest diamond, standing upright, slightly shorter than the kids) jump down smiling onto the stone paving and gather side by side to the right of the minibus. Clean outlines, fluid animation, 16:9.
```

### Plan 3 : Papa Ibou salue au volant (2,5 - 4 s)
```text
2D flat-vector cartoon animation style, cinematic close-up. Papa Ibou, friendly Senegalese driver with a neat black moustache and green cap, wearing an orange and yellow checkered shirt, leans out of the open driver window of the yellow Car Rapide. He smiles warmly and waves his right hand in an affectionate goodbye gesture. Soft blurred colorful background of the sunny Dakar monument esplanade. Expressive cartoon animation, 16:9.
```

### Plan 4 : Les enfants disent au revoir et le car repart (4 s)
```text
2D flat-vector cartoon animation style, clean contours, warm tones. Medium-wide rear-three-quarter shot. In the foreground, Moussa (yellow t-shirt, orange shorts), Fatou (blue dress), and Palou the upright lion stand seen from behind on the plaza, happily waving with raised arms. Ahead of them, the yellow and blue Car Rapide accelerates gently, Papa Ibou's arm waving out the window, and veers smoothly left along the paved curve toward the foot of the hill into the distance. Dynamic motion, clear perspective, 16:9.
```

### Plan 5 : Marche vers le monument (4 s — fondu au noir)
```text
2D flat-vector cartoon animation style, warm afternoon sunlight. Wide shot from behind. The Car Rapide has left the frame. Moussa, Fatou, and Palou the lion (walking upright on two hind legs, tail gently swishing) walk side-by-side away from the camera across the wide paved esplanade straight toward the foot of the grand stone staircase of the African Renaissance Monument. Steady confident walking cycle, camera slowly pulls back, smooth fade to black in the final second. 16:9.
```
