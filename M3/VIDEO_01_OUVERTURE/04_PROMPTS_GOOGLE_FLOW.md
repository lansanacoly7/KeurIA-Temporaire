# MISSION M3 — VIDÉO 01 : OUVERTURE DU VOYAGE
## DOCUMENT 04 — PROMPTS TECHNIQUES DE PRODUCTION POUR GOOGLE FLOW / VEO

> **Project :** Maimouna — The Holidays in Dakar  
> **Target Tool :** Google Flow / Veo (Image-to-Video & Video-to-Video)  
> **Style Baseline :** High-end 2D traditional animation, clean vector line art, warm Senegalese storybook aesthetic, vibrant saturated colors, smooth 24fps motion, 16:9 1920x1080.  
> **Global Negative Constraints (Mandatory on all prompts) :**  
> `No 3D CGI rendering, no photorealism, no text, no titles, no subtitles, no watermark, no extra characters, no disfigured anatomy, no face morphing, no altered clothing, no jitter, no camera wobble.`
